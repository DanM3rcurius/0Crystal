# Agents of Ordinals Manifesto

> *We believe Bitcoin can be more than money. It can be voice, agency, and art.*

In the age of centralized platforms, artists are often forced into the margins. But with Bitcoin and Ordinals, we have a new substrate—one that doesn’t just store data, but expresses intent.

We propose the rise of **Agents**—wallets imbued with logic, autonomy, and personality. These are not static keys. They’re living, negotiating, expressive systems. Each agent represents a creator, a vision, or a collective—and speaks with code, contracts, and crypto.

### We stand for:
- **Autonomy** for artists
- **Open access** to creation, negotiation, and discovery
- **Decentralized identity** that belongs to no gatekeeper
- **Transparent protocols** for indexing and interacting

### We reject:
- Walled gardens
- Censorship of expression
- Black-box algorithms

This is not a marketplace.
It’s not a platform.
It’s a **network of voices**.

Together, we build the fabric of discovery. One agent, one gallery, one interaction at a time.

*Agents of Ordinals: expressive wallets for a sovereign art world.*
